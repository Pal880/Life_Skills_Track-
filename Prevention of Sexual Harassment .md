# Prevention of Sexual Harassment (PoSH)

## Q1. What kinds of behaviour can cause sexual harassment?


1. **Unwanted physical contact:** Touching, grabbing, kissing, or pinching someone without their consent, or standing unnecessarily close to them.
2. **Sexual comments or jokes:** Making sexual remarks, dirty jokes, or unwanted comments about someone's body or appearance.
3. **Unwanted gestures or staring:** Leering, whistling, or making suggestive gestures.
4. **Offensive messages or media:** Sending unwanted sexual emails, texts, pictures, or videos.
5. **Repeated invitations:** Continuing to ask someone out after they have said no.
6. **Intrusive personal questions:** Asking someone unwanted questions about their sexual or personal life.
7. **Sexist or gender-based behaviour:** Making insulting remarks about someone's gender or treating them unfairly because of it.
8. **Creating a hostile environment:** Behaving in a way that makes the workplace feel intimidating, hostile, or unsafe.

These behaviours should not be ignored. Everyone deserves to feel safe and respected at work.

## Q2. What would you do if you experienced or witnessed one incident or repeated incidents of this behaviour?

If I experienced or witnessed sexual harassment, I would take the situation seriously and respond in a way that is safe and appropriate.

1. **Set a boundary if it is safe:** I would calmly tell the person that their behaviour is unwelcome and needs to stop.
2. **Write down what happened:** I would record the date, time, location, what was said or done, and the names of any witnesses.
3. **Report the incident:** If the behaviour continued, or if the incident was serious, I would report it in writing to a member of the company's Internal Complaints Committee (ICC).
4. **Support the affected person:** If I witnessed the behaviour, I would listen without blaming or judging the person, offer support, and let them decide what help they want.
5. **Seek urgent help for serious incidents:** If there were an assault or an immediate safety concern, I would consider contacting the police or emergency services.
