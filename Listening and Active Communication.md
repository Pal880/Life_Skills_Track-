# Listening and Active Communication

## 1. Active Listening

### Q1. What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?

1. Give full attention to the speaker.
2. Maintain eye contact.
3. Don't interrupt while someone is speaking.
4. Show interest through body language.
5. Ask questions if something is unclear.
6. Avoid judging others.
7. Repeat important points in your own words.
8. Take notes when needed.

## 2. Reflective Listening

### Q2. According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?
Reflective listening means listening carefully and repeating the message to check whether we understood it correctly.

1. **Repeating:** Repeat the important words of the speaker.
2. **Paraphrasing:** Explain the message in your own words.
3. **Reflecting:** Understand and express the speaker's feelings.

We should also observe body language, ask questions and confirm our understanding.

## 3. Reflection

### Q3. What are the obstacles in your listening process?

1. Sometimes I lose concentration.
2. Mobile notifications distract me.
3. Sometimes I think about my reply instead of listening.
4. I sometimes make assumptions.
5. Sometimes I don't understand difficult words.
6. Sometimes I interrupt others.

### Q4. What can you do to improve your listening?

1. Avoid using my phone during conversations.
2. Give full attention to the speaker.
3. Listen patiently.
4. Ask questions if I don't understand.
5. Take notes during meetings.
6. Avoid interrupting others.
7. Practise active listening daily.

## 4. Types of Communication

### Q5. When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day-to-day life?
I use passive communication when:

1. I talk to seniors or experienced people.
2. I am afraid of hurting someone's feelings.
3. I want to avoid arguments.
4. I am not confident about my opinion.

**Example:** Sometimes I stay silent during team discussions even when I have a different opinion.

### Q6. When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day-to-day life?
I become aggressive when:

1. Someone repeatedly interrupts me.
2. Someone does not understand my point.
3. I get frustrated or angry.
4. Someone blames me for my mistakes without understanding the situation.

**Example:** Sometimes I speak loudly when I get angry or frustrated.

### Q7. When do you switch into Passive-Aggressive communication styles in your day-to-day life?
I use passive-aggressive communication when:

1. Someone ignores my suggestions.
2. I feel angry but don't express it directly.
3. Someone repeatedly makes the same mistake.
4. I use sarcasm instead of explaining my feelings.



### Q8. How can you make your communication assertive?
Assertive communication means expressing my thoughts clearly and confidently while respecting others.

I can improve my communication by:

1. Speaking confidently.
2. Using polite and respectful language.
3. Expressing my feelings clearly.
4. Learning to say no when necessary.
5. Controlling my anger.
6. Respecting other people's opinions.
7. Listening to others carefully.
8. Accepting feedback and learning from mistakes.
